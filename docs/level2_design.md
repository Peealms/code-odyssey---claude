# Level 1 on Treasure Island – Variables & Data Types

Status: **built 2026-11-12** - `scenes/levels/level2/level2.tscn`. Players see it as **LEVEL 1**;
the level id is still `level2` (it comes after the tutorial, `level1`). It unlocks once the
tutorial has a star, and the tutorial's CONTINUE button leads here. Update this file when the
design changes.

The lessons are the old Pixel Adventure Level 1's, in Treasure Hunters art: declare a variable of
each basic type (`String`, `int`, `double`, `boolean`, `char`) and change one that already exists
(`name = value;`). It ends with a boss fight against Captain Clown Nose. About 15-20 minutes.

## Building and testing the level
- The map is generated by `tools/build_level2.gd`: one **text map** per room, one character per
  32 px tile (the legend is at the top of the file). Edit a map and re-run - it **overwrites**
  level2.tscn and `resources/tilesets/level2_tileset.tres`:
  `godot --headless --path . --script res://tools/build_level2.gd`
- `tools/test_level2.gd` plays every room and fights the boss (needs a window, not `--headless`):
  `godot --path . --script res://tools/test_level2.gd` - prints 80 PASS/FAIL checks. Each
  obstacle is tried the wrong way first (the wrong hero, the wrong number), then the right way.
  It backs up and restores your save file.
- Rooms are laid out left to right, 1 tile of rock between them, moved up or down so their
  doorways line up. The test reads each room's position from its Room node.

| Room | Code | Hero obstacles | Hazards | Rescues | Coins |

| 3 The ship | `boolean anchorUp = true;`, `boolean hasSword = true;` | low beams (tiny Psalm), the ship, a crab in a crawlspace you can't jump over (fight it with the sword), then a wall-jump shaft to the exit (Chelzy) | the crab, the sea | - | 5 |
| 4 Three paths | `char path = 'B';` | a falling floor in a crawlspace at the end (Kervin's sprint) | Pink Star | - | 5 |

| Exam 2 The low bridge | `bridgeLength = 6;`, then `path = 'C';` | the bridge is under a low ceiling (no jumping; 6 planks is too far to dash), then a wall-jump shaft to the exit (Chelzy) | the sea | - | 5 |
| Exam 3 The treasure | `anchorUp = true;`, then `System.out.println("I can code!");` | Byte's gate of boxes, then Captain Clown Nose (below) | the sea, the captain | - | 3 |

Every hero has a job:

| Hero | Obstacles |
|---|---|
| Chelzy | the shafts in Room 0, Room 3 and Exam 2 |
| Sarah | the Room 0 gap; dodges the captain's swing |
| April | the planks in Room 1 and Room 2; jumps the captain's charge |
| Kervin | the falling floors in Room 2, Room 4 and Exam 1 |
| Psalm | the beams in Room 3 and Exam 1; ducks the captain's thrown sword |

- **Heroes:** the level starts with Chelzy and Sarah (Sarah is rescued in the tutorial). The
  first three rooms each rescue a hero at a flag that needs every coin in its room.
- **`hero` isn't declared yet** when the level starts, so Room 0's line is a declaration. From
  Room 1 on, switching is `hero = "...";` (Byte fixes `String hero = ...` and explains).
- **The exam** is three rooms in the zone `exam`, with the exam music. Code typed anywhere in it
  reaches all three rooms' gadgets. The treasure needs every exam coin.
- **Manual chests:** Variables (Room 0), Numbers (Room 1), Booleans & chars (Room 3), Changing
  variables (Exam 1). Manuals don't carry over from the tutorial, so Room 0 has its own.
- **Spikes** only hurt; nothing sends you back to a checkpoint except falling in the sea
  ("SPLASH!"). Small spikes (`^`) do 20 damage, the big floor and ceiling spikes (`x`, `V`) 50.
  At 0 HP you're back at the last checkpoint.
- **Hints** work as before: 3 wrong lines = the room's hint and Show answer (costs a star),
  5 = the line with blanks.

## Fighting
- `boolean hasSword = true;` puts the sword in the hotbar; hold it (its number key) and
  left-click to swing (10 damage). Byte's first tip in the level says so, and Room 3's crab
  blocks a crawlspace, so every player gets the sword before the boss.
- Crabs and Pink Star (`scripts/enemies/crabby.gd`) patrol, pinch and hurt on touch (20 damage).
  They take 3 hits. Out in the open you can also just jump over them.

## The final boss: Captain Clown Nose (`scripts/level2/captain.gd`)
Exam 3: after Byte's gate ("I can code!") and a checkpoint, you drop into his arena. The
treasure chest appears only when he's beaten.
- **The fight starts** when a hero with the sword walks two tiles into the arena. Without a
  sword he pushes you back and Byte says `hasSword = true;`.
- **During the fight** Byte hides and the terminal is locked ("NO TIME TO TYPE! HOLD Q TO
  SWITCH HEROES"). The hero wheel still works: hold Q, the game slows down, pick a hero. Every
  switch writes `hero = "...";` in Byte's log, so the fight is the lesson about changing a
  variable, under pressure.
- **He picks an attack by distance and shouts it 1.2 s before it lands:**

| He shouts | When you're | Attack | Dodge |
|---|---|---|---|
| CHARGE! | more than 5 tiles away | runs across the arena, sword raised 64 px high (a normal 50 px jump hits it) | April's double jump |
| CATCH THIS! | 2-5 tiles away | the sword spins across at head height (15-99 px up), sticks in the wall for 1.2 s, comes back | tiny Psalm (hold Down) |
| SWING! | 2 tiles or less | a wide swing in front of him | Sarah's dash away |

- **After each attack he's open** (he glows): DIZZY! after a charge (1.6 s, at the wall), MY
  SWORD! while it's stuck in the wall, WHOA! after a swing (1.2 s). Only then does your sword
  hurt him. At other times it bounces off ("HA! MISSED!").
- **60 HP = 6 hits.** His hits do 25 damage (4 hits and you're back at the checkpoint before the
  arena, and he resets with full health). Leaving the arena also resets him.

## Why the numbers changed from the old Level 1
Treasure Hunters tiles are 32 px (the old ones were 16 px), and a hero is 24 px tall:
- **Bridges** are whole planks: 8 in Room 1 (9 jams and collapses; 5.5 is an int error) and 6 in
  the exam. Both are wider than Sarah's best jump-and-dash or dash.
- **jumpPower** is in pixels, so the code is unchanged (1.0 = 50 px, 2.0 = 100 px; a double jump
  adds 50 px). Room 1's planks are 96 px up (only a double jump), Room 2's 128 px (100 px at 1.0,
  150 px at 2.0).
- **Shafts** are 5 tiles (160 px): too high for April even at 2.0, and short enough for
  Chelzy's stamina.
- **Psalm's gaps are beams:** a beam fills the top 14 px of a tile, leaving 18 px - too low for a
  24 px hero, enough for tiny Psalm (12 px).
- **Crawlspaces are 1 tile high** (32 px): you fit, but you can't jump.
- **One-way planks catch you if you're close:** a hero falling while overlapping a plank's top
  edge is pushed up onto it. That's why April's planks are at least 3 tiles up: a normal jump
  (50 px) stays well under them.

## How it stops shortcuts
- Crawlspaces stop jumping over gaps and falling floors.
- April's planks float over open water or open floor with no wall near them, so Chelzy has
  nothing to climb.
- Shafts are taller than anyone but Chelzy can climb.
- `tools/test_level2.gd` tries each obstacle with the wrong heroes and settings.

The `_island` and `_flag` scenes **inherit** the tutorial's scene and only swap the art, so the
script is the same in both levels. The Treasure Hunters sprite frames they use are in
`assets/level2_treasure_hunters/gadgets/`.

The level script `scripts/level2/level2.gd` sets the HUD to COINS, gives Byte's sword tip and
runs the boss fight's start and end: hiding Byte, locking the terminal (`CodeTerminal.locked_reason`)
and showing the treasure.
